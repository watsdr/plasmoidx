## 1. What the Embry-Riddle test actually measured
An independent university meter put the Thunderstorm Generator retrofit to the test. Result: about 9–10% better fuel efficiency and about 34% lower average emissions, under the conditions tested. Real, and far smaller than the 90% claims you may have seen. Plain-English breakdown:
https://plasmoidx.com/explainers/embry-riddle-thunderstorm-generator-test/

## 2. Does the Thunderstorm Generator work?
Does the Thunderstorm Generator work? Short answer: a meter saw a modest, real effect (~+9–10% fuel efficiency, ~−34% average emissions). The bigger claims — 90% savings, near-zero exhaust, water as fuel — are not shown by that test. Claim by claim:

## 3. What is a plasmoid?
What is a plasmoid? Picture a smoke ring that holds itself together — a small, donut-shaped bit of plasma (electrically charged gas) in its own magnetic bottle. What the inventor says it does, and what has actually been measured:
